Dance

Dance has been a part of my life since I was young. I grew up doing competitive dance through high school, competed nationally, and also trained in classical Indian dance. Dance has always been a way for me to stay connected to my culture while expressing myself and doing something I genuinely enjoy.

Yoga

Yoga has become one of my favorite ways to challenge myself while also slowing down. I especially love hot yoga and hope to eventually teach it to others. In April, I will be traveling to Bali, Indonesia to complete my yoga teacher certification and learn more about the practice.

One resource I recommend for learning more about yoga is Yoga Journal .

Traveling

I love traveling because it gives me the chance to experience new places, people, and cultures. I recently backpacked through eight cities in Portugal over two weeks, and in December I plan to begin a backpacking trip through Southeast Asia. Some of my favorite memories come from exploring somewhere completely new.

One of my favorite resources for learning about new destinations is Lonely Planet .

Cooking

Cooking is one of my favorite ways to relax. I love trying new recipes, especially Thai food, but I enjoy making almost anything. I also love having potlucks with my friends because cooking is even better when I get to share what I make with other people.
