a little about me

People, technology & impact.

I'm interested in the space where people, data, and technology come together to solve meaningful problems.

My Background

I'm a senior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studying Information and Data Science with a minor in Communications. My experiences in college have allowed me to explore technology while also developing as a leader.

On campus, I serve as President of Phi Chi Theta, a professional business fraternity. I have also held leadership roles in Alpha Chi Omega and worked as a campus ambassador for brands including LineLeap, Edikted, and Evry Jewels.

One value that has remained important to me is giving back to the communities I'm part of. My family taught me the importance of helping others, and I try to carry that into the organizations, teams, and communities I join.

Career Interests

Through internships in software engineering, data engineering, analytics, and digital technology, I've learned that I'm most interested in building technology that people can actually use. I enjoy understanding both the technical side of a problem and the people who will ultimately interact with the solution.

After graduation, I will continue developing those skills through Kohler's Leadership Development Program, where I will gain experience across different areas of technology.

Looking Forward

Long term, I hope to continue learning about user experience, human-computer interaction, and the relationship between people and technology. I am especially interested in how thoughtful technology can solve real problems and better serve communities that are often overlooked.
