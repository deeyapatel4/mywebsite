My Background

I am a senior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studying Information and Data Science with a minor in Communications. Outside of the classroom, I serve as President of Phi Chi Theta, a professional business fraternity, and previously led PR and Marketing for Alpha Chi Omega. I have also worked as a campus ambassador for brands including LineLeap, Edikted, and Evry Jewels.

Community has always been important to me. My family taught me to give back to the people who give so much to you, and that has shaped the way I lead. Whether through service, leadership, or simply showing up for others, I want to leave my communities better than I found them.

Career Interests and Skills

My internships have given me experience across software engineering, data engineering, analytics, and digital technology. Through those experiences, I realized that I am most interested in where people and technology meet. I enjoy understanding a problem, working with others, and using data and technology to find a solution people will actually use.

My Goals

After graduation, I will join Kohler's Leadership Development Program, where I will rotate through different areas of IT and continue exploring where I want to specialize. Long term, I want to build technology that solves problems people actually have and find ways to use technology to better serve underserved communities.

One day, I would also love to pursue higher education in UX design and study how people interact with technology and products. Ultimately, I want to combine my technical background with my understanding of people to build things that are useful, accessible, and made with a purpose.
